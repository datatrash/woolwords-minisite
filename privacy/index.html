Privacy policy.

Effective date: November 9, 2026 · Publisher: datatrash

This policy explains how datatrash (“we”, “us”) handles information when you use the Woolwords Android game and visit this website. Questions or privacy requests? Email support@datatra.sh.

Information stored on your device

Woolwords generates puzzles on your device and saves information such as your puzzle progress, discovered words, bonus-puzzle unlocks, whether you have bought Remove ads, daily play streaks, earned cosmetic rewards, and sound and appearance preferences in the app’s local storage. The game does not require a Woolwords account. Your Android backup settings may allow Google to back up and restore app data to your Google account.

Advertising and consent

The game uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display a banner and, if you choose, a rewarded ad to unlock a bonus puzzle. It also uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to manage applicable privacy choices. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as your IP address, device and advertising identifiers, app interactions, ad interactions, and diagnostic information to provide and measure ads, prevent fraud, and support security. What is processed and whether ads are personalized depend on your location, device settings, consent choices, and Google’s configuration.

Where required, the app presents a consent message and provides a privacy choices option in its settings. You can also manage advertising preferences through your device and Google account. Learn more in Google’s Privacy Policy and Google’s information about advertising partners.

Purchases

Woolwords offers an optional one-time “Remove ads” purchase through Google Play. Google processes the payment under its own terms and privacy policy; we do not receive your payment details. The app asks Google Play whether you own the purchase and stores the result on your device. After you buy Remove ads, the app no longer loads ads or shows the advertising consent message.

Information you send us

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use it to respond, provide support, and handle your request. Please avoid sending sensitive information that is not needed to explain your question.

This website

This site is a static informational site and does not itself use analytics or advertising scripts or set tracking cookies. Your language preference may be stored locally in your browser. The hosting provider may process standard server information, such as IP addresses, request details, and timestamps, to operate and secure the site. Actual hosting practices depend on the provider used to deploy it.

How information is shared and protected

We share information with Google and its advertising partners as described above when the advertising and consent services operate. Your email provider and ours process messages you send us. Google may process Android backups, and Google Play processes purchases, under its own terms. Our service providers process information to operate and secure their services. We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Retention and deletion

Game data stays on your device until you clear the app’s storage or uninstall it; a backup may restore some data if you choose to restore it. Backups can be managed through your Android and Google account settings. Google and its advertising partners retain information under their own policies. We keep support correspondence for as long as reasonably needed to address the issue and meet applicable obligations, then delete it when no longer needed.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. To contact us about information we hold, email support@datatra.sh. For data controlled by Google, you can use your Google Account controls or contact Google. We may need information to verify or understand a request.

Audience

Woolwords is intended for adults and is not designed or directed to children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information through a support message, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the game, the site, or our practices. We will update the effective date on this page when we do. Contact support@datatra.sh with questions.
