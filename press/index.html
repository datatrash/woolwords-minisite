About

Woolwords is a cozy daily word puzzle for Android. Letters are connected by paths: trace a word from tile to tile and watch the board open up as you find the words hidden inside it. Shared letters stay until no unsolved word needs them.

Facts

  • Platform: Android 7.0 or newer, free on Google Play
  • A fresh puzzle every day, generated on the device; works offline
  • Classic (4×4) and Tough (5×5) boards, no timer
  • Playable in 19 languages, including English, Spanish, French, German, Dutch, Italian, Portuguese, Japanese and Ukrainian
  • One shared daily streak with permanent cosmetic rewards, shown on a stitched yarn trail
  • Free with ads; optional rewarded ad for a bonus puzzle, or a one-time “Remove ads” purchase
  • Made by datatrash
